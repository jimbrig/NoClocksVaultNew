# Definition - Mathematical Universe Hypothesis
## Overview

The **Mathematical Universe Hypothesis (MUH)** is Max Tegmark's proposal that physical reality **IS** a mathematical structure, not merely described by one. The physical universe doesn't "use" mathematics—it literally is mathematics, specifically a mathematical structure encoding relationships and constraints.

## Key Concept

Before anything can manifest physically, there must exist **the mathematical structure defining what states are possible and how they relate**. Physical laws are constraints that actualize specific possibilities from broader possibility spaces—they eliminate degrees of freedom, restricting what can exist.

In quantum mechanics, this structure is the Hilbert space—an abstract vector space where every possible state of a system corresponds to a vector. The field itself is not "made of" anything—it's the **mathematical encoding of all possible configurations and their probability amplitudes**.

## Details

### The Constraint Hierarchy

Physical emergence follows this structure:

1. **Most fundamental level**: Pure mathematical possibility space (unconstrained)
2. **Physical laws**: Constraints specifying relationships, eliminating possibilities
3. **Constants of nature**: Further constraints specifying parameter values ($c$, $G$, etc.)
4. **Initial conditions**: Additional constraints actualizing specific states
5. **Emergent structures**: Higher-level constraints arising from lower-level ones

Each level is **information that restricts what can exist at the next level**.

### Level IV Multiverse

Tegmark's Level IV multiverse proposes that **all self-consistent mathematical structures exist**. Our universe is one particular structure, and observers within it (like us) are "self-aware substructures" experiencing that mathematical structure from the inside as physical reality.

### Logical vs Temporal Priority

The mathematical structure doesn't come "before" the physical universe temporally—it **IS** the universe, viewed from the proper perspective. This is **logically prior**, not temporally prior.

## Implications

1. **Physical reality = mathematical structure**: Not metaphor but identity
2. **Laws as information**: Physical laws encode constraints that reduce possibility space
3. **Existence requires differentiation**: Things must be distinguishable from non-existence
4. **Information is logically prior**: Encoded constraints must exist for manifestation

## Connection to Wheeler

This aligns with Wheeler's ["It from Bit"](Definition - It From Bit.md): the physical world has at bottom an immaterial, mathematical source. Both propose that **information/mathematics is more fundamental than matter**.
