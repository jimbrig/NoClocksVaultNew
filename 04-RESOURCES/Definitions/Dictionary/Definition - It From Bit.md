# Definition - It From Bit
## Overview

**"It from Bit"** is a phrase coined by physicist John Archibald Wheeler proposing that information is fundamental to physics—that every physical entity derives its existence from binary choices and information, not the reverse. Wheeler argued that physical reality ("it") emerges from informational acts ("bit").

## Key Concept

Wheeler's formulation directly inverts the traditional materialist worldview:

> "Every item of the physical world has at bottom—at a very deep bottom, in most instances—an immaterial source and explanation... Particles, fields, even spacetime itself derive their function, meaning, and very existence entirely from the apparatus-elicited answers to yes-or-no questions, binary choices, bits."

This represents a radical ontological claim: **physical things are manifestations of information**, not the other way around.

## Details

### The Participatory Universe

Wheeler conceived of a "participatory universe" where reality doesn't exist in a definite state until observation occurs. He compared the universe to a game of "Twenty Questions" where:
- No one thinks of an answer beforehand
- Each person answers consistently with previous responses
- The "answer" only comes into existence through the questioning process itself

This suggests that **"something" emerges from "nothing" through the act of observation**—through information extraction.

### Quantum Mechanical Foundation

The concept aligns with quantum mechanics:
- Before observation, an electron is neither wave nor particle—it exists in "indeterminate limbo"
- Measurement projects superposition states onto eigenstates, yielding binary outcomes
- "Not until you start asking a question, do you get something"

### Beyond Metaphor

Modern physics increasingly supports this view:
- **Quantum fields**: Probabilistic information structures from which particles manifest
- **DNA**: Linear information encoding that physically manifests as organisms
- **Computer simulations**: Abstract code generating seemingly physical virtual worlds

## Implications

1. **Ontological Reversal**: Matter and energy may not be fundamental—information is
2. **Substrate Independence**: Information transcends specific physical implementations
3. **Observer Participation**: Reality requires observation to actualize
4. **Binary Naturalness**: Binary encoding may reflect quantum measurement structure

## The Philosophical Question

Wheeler's framework addresses "why is there something rather than nothing":
- The **"why"** remains unanswerable
- But the **"what"** (the "something") may be fundamentally **informational**
- Reality's "ground floor" might be neither matter nor mind, but the abstract logical structure giving rise to both

## Supporting Evidence

> [!NOTE] Empirical Demonstrations
> We're surrounded by examples of information manifesting as physical reality:
> - Quantum field excitations = particles
> - DNA sequences = organisms
> - Software code = virtual worlds
> - Electromagnetic modulation = sound and images
